Pouring the plate…

All 302 neurons and 95 muscle cells, wired from the real connectome (Cook et al. 2019). With the puppet on, a prescribed wave drives the muscles. With it off, the motor neurons drive them through the real synapses. Strengths are not fitted yet, so the worm lies still until you stimulate motor neurons in the brain view (try all VD or all DB) and watch the plate.
